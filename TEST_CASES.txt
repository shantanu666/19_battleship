Battleship Manual Test Cases
============================

TC-01: Hit a ship cell [Task 2]
Setup: Place a ship on (1, 1) on a Board.
Steps: Fire at (1, 1).
Expected: fire returns True; that ship's damage includes (1, 1).

TC-02: Miss an empty cell [Task 2]
Setup: Place a ship on (1, 1) on a Board.
Steps: Fire at (4, 4).
Expected: fire returns False; no ship damage changes.

TC-03: Reject a repeated shot [Task 2]
Setup: Place any ship on a Board.
Steps: Fire at (1, 1) twice.
Expected: The first shot resolves normally; the second is rejected and does
not alter damage or shot state.

TC-04: Sink one ship while another remains [Task 2]
Setup: Place ships {(1, 1), (1, 2)} and {(4, 4)}.
Steps: Fire at (1, 1), (1, 2).
Expected: The first ship is reported by sunk_ships; all_sunk remains False.

TC-05: Sink the entire fleet [Task 2]
Setup: Place ships {(1, 1), (1, 2)} and {(4, 4)}.
Steps: Fire at (1, 1), (1, 2), (4, 4).
Expected: Both ships appear in sunk_ships and all_sunk becomes True. In a
game, the turn loop ends when either fleet is sunk.

TC-06: Prevent repeated AI shots [Task 3]
Setup: Create an AI with a small board, such as size 2.
Steps: Request a choice until every cell has been selected, then request again.
Expected: Every coordinate is unique; after all cells are tried, choose returns
None without raising an exception.

TC-07: Target near a hit [Task 3]
Setup: Create an AI and record a hit at (1, 1) on a size-3 board.
Steps: Request its next choice.
Expected: It chooses an untried orthogonally adjacent cell: (0, 1), (2, 1),
(1, 0), or (1, 2). It does not choose diagonally adjacent cells while these
options remain.

TC-08: Reject invalid player coordinates [Existing input validation]
Steps: Enter malformed input, then an out-of-range coordinate.
Expected: Each input is rejected, and the game continues without recording a
shot or taking an AI turn.

TC-09: Quit [Existing input handling]
Steps: Enter q at the player prompt.
Expected: The game exits without firing either side's shot.
